Okay so people keep saying that remote work is killing teamwork, and honestly i just don't buy it.

First of all, i have worked remote for three years now and my team ships more than the office teams do, which is kind of funny when you think about it. We talk all day on chat, we know each other super well, and to be fair we waste way less time in pointless meetings.

Honestly, the real problem is not remote work, it is bad managers. A bad manager in an office is still a bad manager at home, they just have fewer places to hide. If a company can't trust people to work from their kitchen, that says a lot about the company and basically nothing about remote work.

Also, i think people forget the commute. Two hours a day, gone, every single day. That is ten hours a week i now spend on sleep and actual life, and i am a way better coworker because of it.

So at the end of the day, remote work is not the villain here. Give people clear goals and leave them alone, and honestly they will surprise you.
