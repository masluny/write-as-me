This report is about the state of our community garden after the spring season, and honestly there is a lot to talk about.

Okay so the good news first. The tomato beds did super well this year, we got almost double last year's harvest, and the new compost system basically paid for itself already. Volunteers showed up every single weekend, which honestly surprised me in the best way.

To be fair, not everything worked. The herb spiral got way too much shade after the fence went up, so most of the basil just gave up on life. Also the water barrels keep clogging, i think the mesh we bought is kind of cheap, and we should replace it before summer.

My suggestions are simple. First of all, move the herbs to the south bed. Second, buy proper mesh, it costs like ten euros more and saves us hours. And also, we should honestly throw a small party for the volunteers, they earned it.

So at the end of the day the garden is in the best shape it has ever been, we just need to fix a few small things before the summer heat arrives.
