Report: Improving the School Canteen

Introduction
The purpose of this report is to evaluate the current state of the school canteen and to propose practical recommendations for improvement. The findings are based on a short survey of students and staff conducted last month.

Current situation
Overall, respondents expressed moderate satisfaction with the canteen. However, several recurring issues were identified. Queues during the lunch break are long, which leaves many students with limited time to eat. In addition, the menu offers few healthy options, and the seating area is often crowded and noisy.

Recommendations
To address these issues, the following measures are recommended:

1. Introduce a pre-order system via the school app, allowing students to collect meals without waiting in line.
2. Expand the menu to include more fresh, balanced meals, such as salads, wraps and vegetarian dishes.
3. Reorganise the seating area and add acoustic panels to create a calmer, more comfortable environment.

Conclusion
Implementing these changes would significantly enhance the canteen experience for both students and staff. A follow-up survey after one term would help assess the effectiveness of the improvements and identify any remaining areas for development.
